Designing Spaces That Connect, Nourish & Endure

At ASVANA STUDIO, we believe that every space tells a story. Our design philosophy combines thoughtful design with natural connections and lasting impact.

  • Thoughtful Design: Every detail serves a purpose
  • Natural Connections: Harmonizing with the environment
  • Lasting Impact: Creating spaces that endure through time

About ASVANA STUDIO

Founded by Ar. Anuja Vidhate, ASVANA STUDIO is a design practice dedicated to creating spaces that inspire, elevate, and endure. With a focus on the intersection of family, nature, and thoughtful design, we craft environments that nurture well-being and sustainability.

Our team brings expertise in architecture, landscape design, site planning, and vastu consultation to deliver integrated solutions that transform spaces into meaningful experiences.
